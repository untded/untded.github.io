The directory does not stand alone. This register lists the external vocabularies and standards that it references, together with the role that each plays here.

| Vocabulary or standard | Role for this registry |
|---|---|
| **ISO 7372:2005 and ECE/TRADE/362** | These are the publication itself, and the registry transcribes its 2005 edition. Official pages: [UNECE](https://unece.org/untded-iso7372), [ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/41237.html). |
| **ISO 6422** (United Nations Layout Key) | ISO 6422 defines the master form. The [UNLK reconstruction](/unlk) plots the bridges of the directory onto its grid, and the zone semantics of `utd:UnlkZone` follow its line-and-position language. |
| **ISO 3535** | ISO 3535 defines the layout-chart grid on which the UNLK is drawn, including the A4 page, the image area and the standard boxes. The registry cites it wherever geometry matters. |
| **UN/EDIFACT D.05B** (EDED and UNCL) | The D.05B directories form the electronic counterpart of the directory. The [alignment guide](/guides/alignment-edifact) documents the tag join and its results. |
| **ISO 11179** | ISO 11179 supplies the naming discipline behind the element names (object class, property term and representation term), as the foreword of the publication records. |
| **ebXML CCTS (ISO/TS 15000-5)** | ebXML CCTS forms the core-components framing to which the 2005 revision aligned its entries. The registry records it as context and does not consume it as data. |
| **UN Transparency Protocol (UNTP)** | UNTP is an interoperability target rather than a source, because a product-passport payload can cite TDED elements by IRI, as the [context guide](/guides/context) describes. The registry imports no content from it. |

## Policy

1. The registry consumes exactly one source, which is the text of the publication, digitized and verified as the [provenance note](/guides/provenance) describes.
2. External vocabularies appear as citations, alignments or interoperability examples, and never as inputs to the data.
3. Every alignment claim, such as the EDIFACT join, is generated from the data and is reproducible from the dataset repository.
