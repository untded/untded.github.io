The guides describe how this registry is built, the model it publishes, and the ways a reader can use or correct the data. Each guide addresses one part of the work:

- The [ontology guide](/guides/ontology) describes the RDF model of the directory and gives worked examples.
- The [UN/EDIFACT alignment guide](/guides/alignment-edifact) records how the directory and the D.05B directories corroborate each other, element by element.
- The [vocabulary register](/guides/vocabulary-register) lists the external vocabularies and standards the directory references.
- The [provenance note](/guides/provenance) describes how the digitization was made and verified.
- The [JSON-LD context guide](/guides/context) describes how a JSON-LD document cites TDED terms.
- The [contributing guide](/guides/contributing) describes how corrections and new editions are requested.

The registry pages carry the data themselves: the [directory](/elements), the [ontology](/ontology), the [edition changes](/changes), the [bridges map](/bridges) and the [UN layout key reconstruction](/unlk).
